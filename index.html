FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before making room for the work that matters.

Will HappyCalendar change my calendar when I'm not looking?

Never silently. Every proposed change is shown to you for approval first, and you can toggle any behaviour off at any time. You stay in control of every event on your calendar.

Which calendar and task tools does it support?

HappyCalendar works with Google Calendar and Outlook, and syncs tasks from Notion, Jira and more. It sits on top of the tools you already use — there's no new inbox to babysit.

Will it decline meetings on my behalf?

Only if you choose to. Meeting veto looks at an invitation against your protected time and, when you've enabled it, declines or proposes a better slot automatically. You can review and override every action.

Can I cancel or change plans anytime?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever with no card required, and paid plans can be cancelled or downgraded any time from settings. Your data and deep-work blocks are never held hostage.

How is my data protected and who owns it?

Your calendar data belongs to you. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, we're GDPR compliant, and we never sell or share your data with third parties. You can export or delete it at any time.

Do you offer discounts for teams or non-profits?

Yes — we offer annual savings (20%), plus discounted and free plans for education, non-profits and early teams. Get in touch and we'll find a plan that works for how you work.
